Shipping & Refunds

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Everything is made fresh per order, so this policy is a little different from a policy for socks. The short version: if what arrives is damaged, wrong or missing, tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we will replace it or refund you in full. Because it is fresh food, we cannot take back an opened or eaten product simply because you changed your mind.

1. Where we ship

We currently ship across India. We do not ship internationally yet. Some remote PIN codes may be unserviceable by our courier partners — if yours is, we will contact you and refund you in full.

2. Dispatch and delivery times

  • Dispatch: within 1–2 business days of order confirmation, since each order is made fresh.
  • Delivery: typically 3–5 business days after dispatch, depending on your location.
  • Tracking: emailed to you as soon as your parcel leaves us.

These are good-faith estimates, not guarantees. Courier delays, weather and public holidays can push them out.

3. Shipping charges

  • Orders of ₹499 and above: free shipping.
  • Orders below ₹499: a flat ₹80 shipping charge, shown at checkout before you pay.

4. Chocolate in transit

Chocolate is heat sensitive. In summer or in transit through hot regions it can soften or develop a pale “bloom” on the surface. Bloom is cosmetic. It is safe to eat and does not affect flavour. We pack with insulation where we can, but we cannot guarantee against it, and cosmetic bloom on its own is not treated as damage.

If a piece arrives melted out of shape, cracked, or broken, that is damage and it is covered — see below.

5. If something is wrong with your order

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@nomarksleft.com with your order number and a photograph of the item and the packaging, if:

  • the product arrived broken, crushed or melted out of shape;
  • you received the wrong flavour or the wrong quantity;
  • an item is missing from your parcel;
  • the product is past its best-before date on arrival.

We will verify and then offer you, at your choice, a free replacement or a full refund including the shipping you paid. We will not ask you to post the food back to us.

6. Change-of-mind returns

Because our products are perishable food made fresh to order, we cannot accept returns or issue refunds simply because you changed your mind, disliked the flavour, or ordered the wrong thing. This is standard for food and is permitted under Indian consumer law for perishable goods. It does not affect your rights where a product is defective, damaged, or not as described — those are covered in full above.

7. Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch: email us and we will cancel and refund you in full, no questions asked.
  • After dispatch: we cannot cancel, because the item has already been made and handed to the courier.

8. Failed or duplicate payments

If a payment fails, you are not charged. Occasionally a bank places a temporary hold that looks like a charge on your statement; these are released automatically, usually within 5–7 business days. If you were genuinely charged twice, or charged for an order you never received a confirmation for, email us and we will refund the full amount.

9. Refund timelines

Once a refund is approved, we process it within 2 business days. It is then credited to your original payment method, which typically takes a further 5–7 business days depending on your bank or card issuer. We will email you when we initiate it.

10. Undelivered and returned parcels

If a parcel comes back to us because the address was incomplete, nobody was available across repeated delivery attempts, or delivery was refused, we will refund the order value but not the original shipping cost. Please double-check your address and phone number at checkout.
