Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data No Marks Left collects when you use this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can do about it. We have written it in plain language rather than legalese, because a privacy policy you cannot read is not much of a policy.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by [[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]] (“No Marks Left”, “we”, “us”), with its place of business at [[REGISTERED ADDRESS]], India. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data.

2. What we collect

We only collect what the site actually needs. Here is the complete list:

WhatWhenWhy
Email address When you join the waitlist, sign up for updates, or enter it at checkout To notify you about launch, restocks and order status
Name, shipping address, phone number Only if you type them into the checkout form To deliver an order and contact you about it
Usage data — pages viewed, products clicked, items added to cart, scroll depth, buttons pressed Automatically, as you browse To understand which products people want and where the site confuses them
Session recordings — a replay of your mouse movement, clicks and scrolling on the page Automatically, as you browse To find broken or confusing parts of the site. All text you type into any field is masked and is not visible in these recordings.
Device and technical data — browser, operating system, screen size, referring website, approximate city-level location derived from your IP address Automatically To make the site work across devices and to see where visitors come from
Cart contents When you add something to your cart Stored locally in your own browser so your cart survives a page refresh

3. What we do not collect

We never collect, transmit or store your card number, expiry date or CVV. Card details are handled only by our payment processor at the moment of payment. Nothing you type into a payment field is sent to our servers, saved in our analytics, or visible in session recordings.

We also do not collect government ID numbers, and we do not buy personal data about you from anyone else.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use two kinds of browser storage:

  • Essential storage. Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage so it is not lost when you reload. This never leaves your device.
  • Analytics cookies. Set by PostHog to recognise a returning visitor and avoid counting the same person twice. These do not identify you by name.

You can clear both at any time through your browser settings. Clearing them empties your cart but does not otherwise break the site. If you use your browser's “do not track” setting or an ad blocker, analytics will simply not load.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • PostHog — product analytics and session recording. Data is processed on servers in the United States.
  • Formspree — receives and stores the email address you submit through our forms.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and processes standard server request logs, including IP addresses.

Because some of these providers are outside India, your data may be transferred and stored abroad. We rely on the contractual protections these providers offer to their customers. We may also disclose data if we are legally required to by a court or authority.

6. How long we keep it

  • Email addresses: until you ask us to remove you, or until you unsubscribe.
  • Analytics and session recordings: retained for a rolling period of up to 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Order and address details: for as long as needed to fulfil the order and meet tax and accounting obligations.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data of yours we hold;
  • correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete your personal data;
  • withdraw a consent you previously gave, at any time;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to.

Email us at hello@nomarksleft.com and we will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for any of this. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your request, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use HTTPS across the whole site, keep personal data access limited to people who need it, and rely on established providers rather than rolling our own storage. No system is perfect, and we cannot promise absolute security — but if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant and we hold your email address, we will tell you directly.
